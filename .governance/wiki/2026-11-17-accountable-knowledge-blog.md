# 2026-11-17 — What software engineering and customer support had figured out

Post: `blog/posts/2026-11-17-what-engineering-and-support-figured-out.md`

## Argument

Software engineering and customer support show AI gains partly because they already made knowledge contextual, accountable, attributable, composable, on-demand, actionable and auditable. Elsewhere, those connections live in people. The post links this to the book *Accountable Knowledge Architecture for Enterprise AI* (Accountable Knowledge Units, Knowledge Context Packages, exchange protocol, separate action gateway).
